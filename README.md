# About Me:
I am a VLSI Design & Verification Engineer specializing in digital RTL design and UVM-based verification. With a strong foundation in SystemVerilog, UVM, and industry-standard EDA tools, I focus on building reusable verification testbenches, hunting corner-case protocol bugs, and achieving complete coverage closure.<br><br>🛠️ What I Bring to the Table<br>- Design & Verification: RTL Design, UVM Environment Architecture, Constrained-Random Verification, and Coverage-Driven Verification.<br>- Protocol Competency: Hands-on experience modeling and verifying AMBA AXI3, APB, and SPI interfaces.<br>- EDA & Debug: Simulation, waveform debug, linting, and synthesis using QuestaSim, Xilinx Vivado, Synopsys Design Compiler, and SpyGlass.<br><br>🚀 Key Project Highlights<br>- AXI3 Verification IP (VIP): Architected a modular UVM environment containing active/passive agents, constrained-random burst transaction sequences, and scoreboard checkers to catch handshaking anomalies.<br>- APB-Based SPI Controller: Designed the synthesizable Verilog RTL and built an end-to-end SystemVerilog/UVM testbench with SVA protocol assertions and functional coverage metrics.<br><br>Feel free to explore my repositories below or connect with me to discuss silicon verification, digital design, or full-time opportunities!
